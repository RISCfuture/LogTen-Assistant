Where the evidence comes from

Comparisons against the aircraft need evidence of what the aircraft actually did. The Data Sources page, the first in Audit & Fix, shows where that evidence comes from and how much of your flying it covers.

The three kinds of data

  • FlySto parses the data logs your avionics recorded. See Sync flights from FlySto.
  • ADS-B traces come from the public adsb.lol archive. See Fetch ADS-B traces.
  • Airport Data is FAA reference data used to check airports, approaches and night. See Update FAA airport and approach data.

Coverage

The Coverage table shows, for each aircraft you’ve flown, how much of its flying the evidence covers. A flight with no evidence can’t be compared, so it produces no Aircraft Comparisons findings; that’s a gap in coverage, not a clean bill of health.

Running tasks

A task in progress appears at the top of the page, with a Stop button. Audit ▸ Stop (⌘.) does the same. Only one data task runs at a time.

When data gets old, the app offers to update it and asks Update or Not Now.
