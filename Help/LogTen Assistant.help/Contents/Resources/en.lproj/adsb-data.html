Fetch ADS-B traces

ADS-B is the broadcast most aircraft send about their own position. The community site adsb.lol archives it every day. LogTen Assistant downloads the days you flew and keeps only your own aircraft’s traces.

Fetch traces

  1. Choose Audit ▸ Fetch ADS-B Data…, or click Fetch… on the Data Sources page.
  2. Set From and To: up to a month at a time.
  3. Under Aircraft Flown, choose the aircraft. An aircraft needs a known ICAO address; the app works one out from a US N-number, or you can enter one in Logbook Settings ▸ Aircraft.
  4. Click Check Size, then download.

Each day’s archive is several gigabytes, so check the size before you start. The app streams the archive and keeps only the traces of the aircraft you chose; the rest is discarded as it goes. Click Stop at any time.

Where the traces go

Traces are stored in a cache. Settings ▸ Data shows its location, with Show in Finder, and Keep up to caps its size.

The traces come from the adsb.lol archive, under the Open Database License (ODbL).
