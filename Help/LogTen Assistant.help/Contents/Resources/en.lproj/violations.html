Find flights that break currency rules

Violations lists the flights that break the pilot-currency rules of 14 CFR part 61. It is a report: unlike Audit & Fix, it never proposes a correction and never changes anything.

Read the list

Choose All Violations in the sidebar for every flight at once, or one of the rules beneath it. The sidebar badge counts that rule’s violations. Select a flight to see in the inspector which requirement it missed.

The rules are:

  • Flight Review — 14 CFR 61.56(c)
  • Passenger Currency — 61.57(a)
  • Night Passenger Currency — 61.57(b)
  • IFR Currency — 61.57(c)
  • Proficiency Check and Proficiency Check in Type — 61.58(a)(1) and (a)(2)
  • NVG Currency and NVG Passenger Currency — 61.57(f)
  • Instruction Limit — 61.195(a)
  • Instructor Time in Type
  • SIC Currency

Point at a rule in the sidebar for its citation and for what the check can’t see.

If a rule says “Not Checked”

A rule doesn’t run when it can’t. The page says why — the rule is turned off, applies only to flight instructors or instrument-rated pilots, or needs a field mapped in Logbook Settings — and its button takes you straight to the setting that lets it run. You can also Control-click the rule in the sidebar. See Change the settings for one logbook.

Make the result accurate

The report is only as good as your pilot profile. Set your certificate, ratings and home time zone in Logbook Settings, and record any currency event the logbook doesn’t show — such as a flight review logged elsewhere — under Rules ▸ Confirmed Currency Events.

LogTen Assistant is an aid for reviewing your own records. It does not decide whether you were legal to fly.
