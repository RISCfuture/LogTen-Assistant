Update FAA airport and approach data

Some checks need to know where airports are, what time zone they’re in, and what approaches they have. That comes from FAA data, which is published in 28-day cycles.

Airport data (NASR)

Choose Audit ▸ Update NASR Data, or click Update beside NASR Cycle on the Data Sources page. The app downloads the current cycle from the FAA. To have it update on its own, turn on Update NASR data automatically in Settings ▸ Data.

Approach data (CIFP)

Choose Audit ▸ Update Approach Data, or click Update beside CIFP Cycle. The app downloads the FAA’s coded instrument flight procedures to name the approaches your tracks fly. If you already have the unzipped FAACIFP18 file, add it by hand and the row reads Added by hand.

If the data is out of date, the audit says so with an Airport Data Out of Date finding, and the app offers to update it.
