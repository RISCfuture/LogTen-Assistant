Open a logbook

LogTen Assistant audits a logbook kept in LogTen Pro. Each logbook opens in its own window, so you can keep two side by side. The app is sandboxed: it can read a logbook only after you choose the folder it lives in.

Open your logbook

  1. Choose File ▸ Open Default LogTen Logbook… (⇧⌘O). The Open dialog starts in LogTen’s own folder.
  2. Click Open to give the app access to that folder.

If your logbook is somewhere else, choose File ▸ Open Logbook… (⌘O) and pick its folder. The first window the app shows has the same two choices as buttons: Open Default LogTen Logbook… and Open Other Folder….

Logbooks you’ve opened before appear in that window and under File ▸ Open Recent. Clear Menu forgets them.

LogTen Assistant needs LogTen Pro installed in /Applications. It borrows LogTen’s data model to read the logbook. It never opens your logbook while LogTen is running — see Why the app reads a copy of your logbook.

Find your way around

The sidebar lists everything in the window. Audit & Fix holds the pages for finding and correcting problems, and Violations lists flights that break pilot-currency rules. Each row shows how many findings are waiting for review; rows with nothing to show are gray. ⌘1 through ⌘8 jump to the pages in order.

The toolbar has the commands you’ll use most: Run Audit, Sync FlySto, Apply Fixes… and the inspector toggle. Every one is also in a menu. The Audit, Review and Apply menus carry the rest.

Choose a finding or a flight and the inspector on the right shows its details. Hide it with View ▸ Hide Inspector (⌥⌘I).

Run your first audit

The app audits the logbook as soon as you open it. Choose Audit ▸ Run Audit (⌘R) to take a fresh look after you’ve changed something. To audit only part of the logbook, choose View ▸ Scope and pick Last 90 Days, This Year or All Flights.
