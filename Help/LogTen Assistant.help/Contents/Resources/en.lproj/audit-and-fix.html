What Audit & Fix checks

Audit & Fix compares each logbook entry with the others, with your smart groups, and with what the aircraft recorded. Whatever disagrees becomes a finding that you review. Findings are sorted onto four pages, and each has a severity: Suggestion, Questionable or Discrepancy.

Logbook Consistency

Entries whose own times, counts or references disagree. The checks are grouped into categories that appear under the page in the sidebar:

  • Missing Information — an entry without the times, aircraft or places it needs.
  • Times — times that don’t fit together.
  • Landings & Approaches — takeoff, landing and approach counts that don’t add up.
  • Duplicate Entries — entries that may repeat one another.
  • Crew & Roles — role times that disagree with the crew named.
  • Dates — entries dated in the future.
  • Simulator — simulator time and sessions.
  • Aircraft, People & Places — incomplete or inconsistent records, including airports, positions, elevations and time zones checked against FAA data.

Aircraft Comparisons

Entries that disagree with FlySto or ADS-B evidence for the aircraft: times that differ, counts of takeoffs and landings, night time and night landings, airports, a stop you didn’t log, distance, and approaches the track doesn’t show. These checks only work for flights the data sources cover — see Where the evidence comes from.

Unmatched Activity

Flights in FlySto or ADS-B data that no logbook entry accounts for, and recorded flights that fit more than one entry (Ambiguous Match).

Groups & Currency

Smart groups whose definitions don’t match the requirements they track — for instance an instrument-currency group that counts the wrong approaches, or a night group that doesn’t require full stops.

The other pages

  • Data Sources — sync status, downloads and coverage. See Where the evidence comes from.
  • Approved — the fixes you’ve approved, waiting to be applied.
  • Applied History — batches already applied, with their verification. See Revert an applied batch.

Open a page, then work through its findings as described in Review findings and make decisions.

A check you don’t want can be turned off in Logbook Settings. Turned-off checks report nothing the next time the audit runs. See Change the settings for one logbook.
