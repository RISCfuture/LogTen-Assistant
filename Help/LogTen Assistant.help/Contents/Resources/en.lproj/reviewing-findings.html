Review findings and make decisions

Every finding starts Unreviewed. You decide what each one deserves, and the app remembers: a decision carries forward when you run the audit again, so you aren’t asked about the same finding twice.

Make a decision

Select a finding. The inspector shows the evidence, and the decision buttons sit beneath it:

  • Mark for Correction — the logbook is wrong and the proposed fix is right. The fix goes to the Approved page.
  • Keep as Logged — the logbook is right; the finding is a false alarm.
  • Needs More Evidence — you can’t tell yet.
  • Reset Decision — back to Unreviewed.

Only some findings come with a fix. If a finding has no proposed change, Mark for Correction is dimmed — keep it or set it aside, and change the entry in LogTen yourself.

The same commands are in the Review menu: Mark for Correction (↩), Keep as Logged (⌫), Needs More Evidence (E). Each moves on to the next finding. Next Unreviewed (]) and Previous Unreviewed ([) skip ahead without deciding.

Give a reason

You can record why. Choose a Reason in the inspector — The evidence confirms it, The logbook is right, Another pilot’s flight or partial credit, The evidence is unreliable or Other — and add a Note. Press ⌘↩ in the note to mark for correction with it. Reasons and notes aren’t recorded when several findings are selected at once.

Review several at once

Select more than one finding to decide them together. The inspector tells you how many can be marked for correction, since not every finding has a fix.

Take a different view

Type in the search field to narrow a page. View ▸ Scope limits findings to the Last 90 Days, This Year or All Flights. To take findings out of the app, choose File ▸ Export Findings or Export Review Decisions….

When a finding is marked for correction, nothing has changed in your logbook yet. Continue with Apply fixes to your logbook.
