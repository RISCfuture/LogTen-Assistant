Privacy

LogTen Assistant · Updated 14 November 2026

In short

  • Your logbook is never uploaded. The one exception is data logs you choose to upload to FlySto yourself.
  • There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.
  • Release builds send redacted crash and diagnostic reports to Sentry. Nothing in them identifies you or your flights.
  • Everything else the app contacts is a public download or a service you set up yourself.

What stays on your Mac

LogTen Assistant reads snapshot copies of your logbook folder. The logbook, its snapshots, backups, your review decisions and notes, the history of applied fixes, downloaded traces and FAA data are stored on your Mac. Credentials you enter are kept in your Keychain. The app does not read the logbook until you choose its folder, and never opens LogTen’s live store.

What the app contacts

ServiceWhenWhat is sent
Sentry Release builds, after a crash or a diagnostic event. Crash and diagnostic reports about the app, with personal details redacted. They do not include your logbook, flight data, registrations, names or file paths.
GitHub releases API When the app checks for updates. An ordinary request for the list of public releases. Nothing about you, your Mac or your logbook.
FlySto Only if you add an API token, and you sync, import links or upload. Your API token, to fetch the logs added to your account. If you choose Upload Data Logs to FlySto…, the data log files you select. Nothing else is uploaded.
adsb.lol (via GitHub) When you fetch ADS-B data. Requests for the public daily archives for the days you choose. The app discards everything except your aircraft’s traces as it downloads; which aircraft you chose is not sent.
OpenSky Network Only if you add OpenSky credentials. Your OpenSky client ID and secret, to the OpenSky sign-in service, to authorize requests to its API.
FAA (faa.gov, aeronav.faa.gov) When you update NASR or approach data, or automatically if you turn that on. An ordinary download request for the current cycle.
FAA airmen database Only when you use Look Up… in Logbook Settings. A download request for the public database. The search for your name runs on your Mac; the name is not sent.

Like any download, these requests reveal your IP address to the server that answers them. The app contacts no other servers.

Crash and diagnostic reports

Release builds report crashes and diagnostics to Sentry so the developer can find and fix bugs. Reports describe the app’s own state: the crash, the version of the app and macOS, and the app’s own events. They are redacted before they leave the Mac, and carry no personal information. Debug builds, which are built from source, send nothing.

Third parties

FlySto, adsb.lol, OpenSky Network, the FAA, GitHub and Sentry operate under their own privacy policies. The data you provide to FlySto and OpenSky is governed by your agreement with them.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. For a question, open an issue on GitHub. The source code is public, so every claim here can be checked against it.
